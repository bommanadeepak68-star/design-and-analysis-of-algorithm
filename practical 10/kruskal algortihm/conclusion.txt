Conclusion
Kruskal's algorithm is a greedy algorithm used to find the Minimum Spanning Tree of a connected, weighted, undirected graph. It selects edges in increasing order of weight while avoiding cycles. It uses sorting and the Disjoint Set (Union-Find) technique to efficiently construct the MST.
